Self-Balancing Robot

Solo build · Design · Electronics · Firmware
Fall 2026
Drag to rotate · Scroll to zoom

A two-wheeled inverted pendulum that drives its wheels under its centre of mass to stay upright. I designed the chassis, wired the electronics and wrote all of the control code.

  • Engineered a fully autonomous two-wheeled self-balancing robot that stabilizes an inherently unstable inverted pendulum system in real time
  • Designed a lightweight, modular chassis in SolidWorks and 3D-printed it for fast iteration and easy assembly
  • Integrated and wired the full electronics stack: Arduino microcontroller, MPU-6050 IMU and L298N H-bridge motor driver
  • Developed closed-loop PID control firmware in embedded C++, using complementary-filter sensor fusion to estimate tilt at 200 Hz
  • Added anti-windup, drift compensation and a live serial tuning interface with EEPROM storage, cutting tuning time from full code re-uploads to seconds
