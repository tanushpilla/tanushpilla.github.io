Self-Balancing Robot

Solo build · Design · Electronics · Firmware
Fall 2026
Drag to rotate · Scroll to zoom

A two-wheeled inverted pendulum that drives its wheels under its centre of mass to stay upright. I designed the chassis, wired the electronics and wrote all of the control code.

Engineered a fully autonomous two-wheeled self-balancing robot that stabilizes an inherently unstable inverted pendulum system in real time. Designed a lightweight, modular chassis in SolidWorks and 3D-printed it, with a high centre of mass and a structure optimized for fast iteration and easy assembly. Integrated and wired the full electronics stack: an Arduino microcontroller, an MPU-6050 IMU and an L298N H-bridge motor driver. Developed closed-loop PID control firmware in embedded C++, using complementary-filter sensor fusion to estimate tilt at 200 Hz. Added anti-windup, drift compensation, and a live serial tuning interface that saves settings to EEPROM, which reduced tuning time from full code re-uploads to seconds and made design-test-refine cycles much faster.
